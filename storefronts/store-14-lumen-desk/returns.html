Returns & 60-day guarantee

If a LumenDesk order arrives damaged, wrong, or simply is not what you expected, you have 60 days from delivery to tell us and we will make it right.

How to start a return

  1. Email hampusi97@gmail.com with your order number and a photo if the item is damaged.
  2. We reply within one business day with either a replacement or a return label.
  3. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the item arriving back.

What we cannot take back

Opened consumables (food, drink, skincare) for hygiene reasons, and digital downloads once the file has been delivered — unless the file is faulty, in which case we resend or refund it.

Bulk and institutional orders

Orders over 10 units are covered by the written terms on your quote. Contact us before returning anything in bulk.
